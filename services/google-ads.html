The problem

Most Google Ads accounts burn money.

Google's own "smart" campaigns are built to spend your budget, not convert it. Agencies charge a percentage of ad spend, which means they make more when you waste more. The incentives are broken.

Our job is to get you phone calls and form fills. Not impressions, not clicks, not "brand awareness". Month-to-month management means if the numbers aren't there, nobody's locked into anything.

Who this works for

Local service businesses with a phone number.

Google Ads works best for:

  • Trades (earthworks, civil, building, drainage, tiling, electrical, plumbing)
  • Local service businesses with a well-defined service area
  • Businesses where a single new job covers several months of ad spend
  • Anyone currently relying only on word of mouth or Facebook

Not a great fit: e-commerce, national brands, anyone without a landing page or site that can convert.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Google Ads management runs month-to-month. If the numbers aren't there, we part ways and you keep the account.

What budget do I need?

For most Melbourne trades, a daily ad budget that covers enough clicks to start learning what works. We'll recommend a number on the first call based on your services and service area.

How long until I see leads?

It varies, and we won't promise a number. Apollo's first lead came 42 hours after going live. A tougher market has taken around two months. It comes down to your service, your competition and your budget. Every enquiry is tracked from day one, so you'll see where it stands, and it's month to month if the numbers aren't there.

Who owns the account?

You do. We set it up under your business name from the start. If you stop working with us, the account, history and data stay with you.

Do I need a website first?

Yes. Ads send people somewhere, and that somewhere needs to convert. We build websites too, so we can handle both at once.
