Google Business Profile Melbourne. Get found on the map.

We set up, verify and look after your Business Profile, so you show up when locals search for your trade and your suburb. Fresh posts every week, reviews watched daily, and a direct line to the person doing the work.

The problem

Most profiles get set up once and forgotten.

When someone searches your trade and their suburb, your Business Profile is often the first thing they see, ahead of your website. Your phone number, your reviews and your photos are all right there.

An unverified listing, old photos and months of silence make that first look easy to skip. We keep your profile accurate, active and easy to call from, so you don't have to remember to.

What you get

Every Business Profile engagement.

Setup and verification

We claim or create your profile and work through Google's verification, including the harder cases like rural addresses and businesses with no shopfront.

Profile clean-up

Categories, services, service area, hours, description and photos filled out properly, so Google knows what you do and where.

Two posts a week

Eight posts a month written for your business and scheduled ahead, using your own photos wherever we can.

Review monitoring

New reviews checked daily. A bad one gets flagged to you fast, so you can respond.

Directory listings

Your name, address and phone number set up consistently across the directories that matter locally.

Plain English reporting

Calls, direction requests, website clicks and profile views, explained without the jargon.

Who this works for

Local businesses people search for by suburb.

A managed profile works best for:

  • Trades with a defined service area, with or without a shopfront
  • Workshops, detailers, clubs and local services people look up before they call
  • Businesses with an unverified, empty or out-of-date profile
  • Anyone running a website or Google Ads who wants their map listing working too

It pairs well with a custom website and Google Ads. Not a great fit: online-only businesses with no local customers.

Who owns the profile?

You do. You stay the owner and add us as a manager. If we ever stop working together, you remove us and keep everything.

Do I need a website?

No, but a profile works harder with one, because the website button has to send people somewhere that converts. If you need a site, we build those too.

Google won't verify my business. Can you help?

Often, yes. Verification gets harder for rural addresses and businesses with no shopfront. We have taken a failed video verification through Google support and got it passed, so we know what they ask for.

Can you remove a bad review?

Only Google can remove a review, and only if it breaks their policies. We can flag the ones that do. For the rest, a calm public reply from you and a steady flow of new reviews does more.

Can you show me how each post performed?

No one can. Google retired per-post stats in 2023, so we report at profile level: calls, direction requests, website clicks and profile views.
