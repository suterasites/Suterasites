The challenge

A long-running business with a dated website.

Maribyrnong & Hobsons Bay Reblocking is a family business based in West Footscray. It has 35 years in the trade, a 15-year written guarantee and the registrations that matter on a structural job. Its old website didn't show much of that.

Reblocking usually starts with a worried homeowner: a sloping floor, doors that stick, a crack that keeps growing. The brief was a site that answers those questions and makes it easy to ask for a quote, then ads to put it in front of the people searching.

What we delivered

Site first, then ads, then every suburb.

May 2026

Website rebuild

A custom site with pages for each service, a quote form and click-to-call, live on the client's own domain on 21 May.

May 2026

Google Ads

Campaign live the next day, with its own quote landing pages and conversion tracking on forms and calls.

July to August

23 suburb pages

A reblocking and restumping page for each suburb in its patch across Maribyrnong, Hobsons Bay and the Macedon Ranges, written around the local housing.

September

Ads moved closer to home

Enquiries were coming from the inner and middle west, and the owner wanted ads there too. The campaign moved to Hobsons Bay, with its landing page rewritten to match.
